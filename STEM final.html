🌡️ 1. Chirp-O-Meter: Predicting Temperature with Crickets!

Did you know crickets can be little thermometers with wings? It’s true, dear! Their chirping actually speeds up or slows down depending on the temperature. Crickets are cold-blooded. The warmer it is, the faster they chirp!

🎯 Activity: Count the Chirps!

1. Go outside and find a cricket OR (https://youtu.be/CpGtBnVZLSk?feature=shared add 5sec of play).
2. Count how many chirps you hear in 15 seconds.
3. Add 40 to that number. That gives you the temperature in Fahrenheit!

Example: 30 chirps in 15 seconds → 30 + 40 = 70°F

⏳ 2. Sun Dial DIY: Solar-Powered Time Travel!

Before digital clocks and atomic watches, people used shadows to tell time! With just the sun and a stick, you can make your own sundial.

🎯 Supplies Needed:

  • A paper plate (or a flat surface)
  • A pencil or stick (your gnomon!)
  • A sunny spot
  • A clock for checking time

🔧 Instructions:

  1. Poke the pencil through the center of the plate.
  2. Every hour, mark where the shadow falls and label it with the current time.
  3. Watch as the shadow moves throughout the day like a dancing fairy!
